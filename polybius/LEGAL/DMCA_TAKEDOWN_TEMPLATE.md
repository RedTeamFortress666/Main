US Copyright Office DMCA Designated Agent Directory, or the site's
"Copyright" / "Legal" page). For hosts in the EU use the DSA Art. 16 variant

## Before you send

- [ ] You (or your principal) actually own the copyright. Contributors under
      no CLA may co-own their contributions.
- [ ] The URL points at an unauthorised copy, not commentary, a review, a
      screenshot, or a security write-up quoting snippets (likely fair use).
- [ ] The hashes match the file you examined. Record them in
      `LEAK_RESPONSE_RUNBOOK.md` first.
- [ ] You are prepared for a **counter-notice** under § 512(g). If the uploader
      files one, the host restores the material in 10–14 business days unless
      you sue. Do not send a notice you would not be willing to follow up.
- [ ] Do not include § 1201 anti-circumvention claims in a § 512 notice; they
      are separate and hosts do not process them the same way.

## EU / DSA variant (Art. 16, Regulation (EU) 2022/2065)

For hosting providers in the EU, replace items 4–5 with:

> This notice is submitted under Article 16 of the Digital Services Act. I
> declare in good faith that the information and allegations contained herein
> are accurate and complete. The material identified above is illegal content
> because it infringes copyright in PØLYBĪUS owned by `[RIGHTS HOLDER]` under
> `[Directive 2001/29/EC as implemented in MEMBER STATE]`. I consent to the
> provider forwarding this notice to the uploader as required.

Include a sufficiently substantiated explanation of *why* it is infringing
(what the work is, that no licence exists), the exact URL(s), and your name and
email. Anonymous DSA notices are allowed for some content types but not for
IP claims.

## GitHub specifically

GitHub's DMCA form requires you to state whether the infringing repository is
a **fork** and, for code, to identify the specific files or the whole repo.
Forks are processed via a separate "fork network" statement. If the leaked
copy is of a **private** branch, say so — it goes to the trade-secret angle
in `README.md`.
