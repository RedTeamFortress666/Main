copy of PØLYBĪUS and want it stopped without (yet) going to court. Send by a
method that proves delivery. Review with counsel before sending; in some
jurisdictions an unjustified threat of IP proceedings is itself actionable
(e.g. UK Intellectual Property (Unjustified Threats) Act 2017 — mostly for
patents/trade marks, but be careful with tone).*

## Drafting notes (delete before sending)

- Keep Section 1 to facts you can prove with the evidence bundle from
  `LEAK_RESPONSE_RUNBOOK.md`. No adjectives.
- Pick **one** legal basis per bullet in Section 2 and cite the statute of the
  jurisdiction where the recipient is, not where you are.
- If the recipient is a security researcher who disclosed to you, do not send
  this. Use the security contact process instead.
- Do not threaten criminal referral as leverage; it can be treated as
  improper pressure in several jurisdictions.
- Section 3(7) (Oracle / Enigma / Colossus) is theatrical reservation of
  rights. Do not lead with it in a letter that will be read by a judge.
  Keep it on the physical paper addendum (`PHYSICAL_PAPER.md`) unless
  counsel says otherwise. The Wu-Tang smallprint under **6th rotor
  LLÇ 2026*** is an epigraph, not a cause of action and not an
  affiliation.
