Copy the Doomsday Clock Calendar Vault APK here as:

  DoomsdayClock.apk

Then set the real applicationId in
  permissions/privapp-permissions-polybius.xml
(default placeholder: com.doomsday.clock).

The vault should:
  - Declare android.intent.category.HOME + DEFAULT (it becomes the launcher)
  - Launch com.polybius.polybius/.MainActivity from the covert entry
  - Optionally embed/store the Polybius APK in its own assets if you want
    a single visible app; the ROM still preinstalls both
